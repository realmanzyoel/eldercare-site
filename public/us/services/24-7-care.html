Who it is for

For people who should not be alone, day or night.

Night-time wandering. Falls getting up to the bathroom. A partner who has not slept properly in months. Late-stage dementia, or the last months of life.

24/7 care puts a trained caregiver in the home around the clock, from a rota of a small team, so there is always someone fresh.

Questions

Five questions about 24/7 care.

What is the difference between awake and sleep-over nights?

An awake caregiver stays up all night and is paid for every hour. A sleep-over caregiver has a bed and a protected sleep period, and is woken only occasionally. Sleep-over nights cost less.

Why a team and not one live-in person?

Nobody can care well around the clock for weeks. A team of three to five covers shifts, so everyone is rested and someone always knows them.

How quickly can 24/7 care start?

We usually build up over a few days as the team is introduced. Care starts within 72 hours of signing.

Do caregivers get breaks?

Yes. Breaks and rest are built into the rota, and the law in each state sets minimums we follow.

Can 24/7 care be temporary?

Yes, for example after a hospital stay or at end of life, then step down.
