Meet your caregiver before day one

You meet the main caregiver and the backup caregiver before care starts, at home or on a video call. If it does not feel right, we change the match, no questions asked.

  • Matched on temperament and interests, not only availability
  • A 30-second intro video for each caregiver (being filmed as we hire)
  • A backup who already knows your family
