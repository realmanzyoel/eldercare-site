Licensing

Florida AHCA license number: application in progress. We will not provide care until the license is issued.

Home care in Florida is licensed by the Agency for Health Care Administration (AHCA). Hands-on personal care needs a home health agency license or a nurse registry license, and every license number must appear on marketing materials, which is why ours is in the footer of every page.

  1. 1. Open the AHCA sitequality.healthfinder.fl.gov
  2. 2. Search by nameType Evenhome or our license number
  3. 3. Check statusActive, the license type and the address

Background screening

Every caregiver and every office employee who could reach a client passes Florida Level 2 background screening under Chapter 435, Florida Statutes, through the AHCA Clearinghouse: fingerprints checked against state and FBI records. We do not schedule anyone before the result is clear.

  • Level 2 fingerprint screening (Chapter 435, F.S.)
  • Clearinghouse rescreening and arrest alerts
  • Two references and a work history check
  • Driving record check for anyone who drives clients
  • Identity and right-to-work check

Safety

What we do when something goes wrong.

Written protocols for the four risks families worry about most.

  1. Falls

    Prevent, respond, review

    A home safety walk-through before day one. If a fall happens the caregiver does not lift the person alone, calls 911 if they are hurt, then the family and care manager. We review every fall within 48 hours.

  2. Medicines

    Reminders, not guesswork

    We remind and record; we only assist further where our license and the care plan allow. A missed or wrong dose is reported to the family and the prescriber the same day.

  3. Abuse and neglect

    Speak up, always

    Every worker is trained to recognise and report abuse. Florida Abuse Hotline: 1-800-96-ABUSE (1-800-962-2873), or 911 in an emergency. Reporting is never optional and never punished.

  4. Emergencies

    Clear steps

    Call emergency services first, then the care manager, then the family circle through the app. An incident report follows within 24 hours: what happened and what changed.
