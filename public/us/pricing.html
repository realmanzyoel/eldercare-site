The short answer

Our care starts from USD $30 an hour for companion care, $34 for personal care and $38 for dementia specialist care. Twenty hours a week of dementia care costs about USD $3,293 a month. There is no sign-up fee.

Fees and policies

  • No sign-up or assessment fee
  • Minimum visit of 3 hours
  • Change or stop care with 7 days' notice, no long contracts
  • Weekend and public holiday hours cost about 15% more
  • Your price is confirmed in writing before care starts

What this does not cover

  • Nursing or medical care (we work alongside nurses and doctors)
  • Medical equipment and supplies
  • Mileage over 20 miles a week for errands
  • Meals out, tickets and other costs during outings

Questions

Pricing questions families ask.

Why do you charge more for dementia care?

Dementia specialist caregivers complete extra training and stay with a small team per client. That costs more, and it is what keeps the same faces in the home.

Do you charge for the assessment?

No. The assessment is free and there is no obligation.

How do I pay?

By card or bank transfer, invoiced every two weeks in the family app.

Can insurance or benefits pay?

Often, partly. See paying for care.

Will prices change?

Prices are reviewed once a year, with at least 30 days' written notice.
