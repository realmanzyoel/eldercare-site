The short answer

A good family meeting has a written agenda, the person needing care involved as much as possible, facts about needs and money, and clear decisions with names and dates. Use the eight-point agenda below and keep it under an hour.

Written by The Evenhome care teamClinical review pending: no article is published as medical guidance until a qualified reviewer signs it offLast updated 21 November 2026

The agenda

  1. What mom or dad wants, in their words
  2. What has changed in the last 3 months
  3. Safety worries
  4. Care options and hours
  5. Money: costs and how it will be paid
  6. Who does what (hands-on, admin, visits, money)
  7. What happens in an emergency
  8. When we meet again
Older people talking around a table

Tips

  • Send the agenda beforehand
  • Video-call those far away
  • One person takes notes and shares them
  • Disagree about the plan, not about each other
