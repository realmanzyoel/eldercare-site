Your long-distance checklist

  • Legal: power of attorney, health care surrogate and HIPAA release
  • A list of doctors, medicines, pharmacy and insurance
  • A trusted neighbour with a key
  • A local care manager or Care Director
  • A shared calendar and one family message thread
  • A plan for emergencies: who is called first, second, third
An older couple video calling family on a laptop

Make visits count

  • Check the fridge, mail and medicines
  • Meet the caregiver and care manager
  • Walk the home for safety
  • Spend time just being family

Our care from afar service gives you a named Care Director, the live app, monthly video calls and errands.
