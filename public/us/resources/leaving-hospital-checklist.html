The short answer

The first week home from hospital is when problems are most likely: confusion about medicines, falls, missed follow-ups and not eating. Before discharge, get the medicine list, follow-up appointments, equipment and a named contact; at home, check safety, food and help for the first 72 hours.

Written by The Evenhome care teamClinical review pending: no article is published as medical guidance until a qualified reviewer signs it offLast updated 21 November 2026

Before you leave hospital

  • Written discharge summary and an up-to-date medicine list
  • What changed: new medicines, stopped medicines, new doses
  • Follow-up appointments booked, with transport
  • Equipment ordered: walker, shower chair, commode
  • Who to call with questions, day and night
  • Who will be home on the first night
A white porch with a white front door

The first 72 hours at home

  • Clear paths, rugs up, night lights on
  • Food and drinks within easy reach
  • Medicines in a dosette box, checked by a pharmacist
  • Help with washing and dressing
  • Someone checking in morning and evening

Days 4 to 7

  • Watch for new confusion, fever, pain, breathlessness or not eating
  • Keep the follow-up appointment
  • Decide what ongoing help is needed

Coming home soon? See care after hospital: we aim to start within 72 hours of signing.
