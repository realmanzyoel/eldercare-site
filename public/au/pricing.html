The short answer

Our care starts from AUD $85 an hour for companion care, $102 for personal care and $120 for dementia specialist care. Twenty hours a week of dementia care costs about AUD $10,400 a month. There is no sign-up fee. If you have a Support at Home budget, most of the cost comes from your budget.

Australia: we are not yet providing care. These are planned private-pay prices. For Support at Home clients, prices will align with the published price caps and be set out in your service agreement.

Fees and policies

  • No sign-up or assessment fee
  • Minimum visit of 3 hours
  • Change or stop care with 7 days' notice, no long contracts
  • Weekend and public holiday hours cost about 15% more
  • Your price is confirmed in writing before care starts

What this does not cover

  • Nursing or medical care (we work alongside nurses and doctors)
  • Medical equipment and supplies
  • Mileage over 20 kilometres a week for errands
  • Meals out, tickets and other costs during outings

Questions

Pricing questions families ask.

Why do you charge more for dementia care?

Dementia specialist support workers complete extra training and stay with a small team per client. That costs more, and it is what keeps the same faces in the home.

Do you charge for the assessment?

No. The assessment is free and there is no obligation.

How do I pay?

By card or bank transfer, invoiced monthly in the family app.

Can insurance or benefits pay?

Often, partly. See funding.

Will prices change?

Prices are reviewed once a year, with at least 30 days' written notice.
