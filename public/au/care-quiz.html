The short answer

Eight plain questions, about two minutes. At the end you see a suggested starting plan and hours. We never ask for your email before showing the result.

Question 1 of 8
Who needs care?
How is their memory?
How much help do they need with washing and dressing?
Have there been safety worries in the last 3 months?
How are meals and drinking?
Who helps them now?
Where do you live?
What is happening right now?

This quiz suggests a starting plan. It is not a medical or care assessment and does not replace advice from a doctor. Nothing you choose here is sent anywhere unless you ask us to contact you.

What happens next

  • The result is a starting point, not an assessment
  • A care manager adjusts it after meeting you both
  • You get a written plan and price before anything starts
