Worker screening

Every support worker will hold a current NDIS Worker Screening Check or an aged care police check, as the law requires at the time, and follow the Code of Conduct for Aged Care. Our work will be measured against the strengthened Aged Care Quality Standards.

  • NDIS Worker Screening Check or police check
  • Code of Conduct for Aged Care, signed and trained
  • Two references and a work history check
  • Driver's licence and vehicle check for anyone who drives clients
  • Identity and right-to-work check

Safety

What we do when something goes wrong.

Written protocols for the four risks families worry about most.

  1. Falls

    Prevent, respond, review

    A home safety walk-through before day one. If a fall happens the support worker does not lift the person alone, calls 000 if they are hurt, then the family and care manager. We review every fall within 48 hours.

  2. Medicines

    Reminders, not guesswork

    We remind and record; we only assist further where our registration and the care plan allow. A missed or wrong dose is reported to the family and the prescriber the same day.

  3. Abuse and neglect

    Speak up, always

    Every worker is trained to recognise and report abuse. Serious incidents reported under the Serious Incident Response Scheme (SIRS); 000 in an emergency. Reporting is never optional and never punished.

  4. Emergencies

    Clear steps

    Call emergency services first, then the care manager, then the family circle through the app. An incident report follows within 24 hours: what happened and what changed.
