The short answer

Support at Home is the Australian Government's in-home aged care program, which replaced Home Care Packages and Short-Term Restorative Care from 1 November 2025. You get an assessment through My Aged Care, are assigned one of eight funding classifications, choose a registered provider, and pay a contribution based on your pension status and the type of service.

The steps

  1. Contact My Aged Care online or on 1800 200 422
  2. Have an aged care assessment at home
  3. Receive your notice of decision with your classification
  4. Wait to be assigned funding (there can be a wait)
  5. Choose a registered provider and sign a service agreement
  6. Plan services within your quarterly budget

Classifications and budgets

There are eight ongoing classifications. As published for July 2026, yearly budgets run from about $11,010 at level 1 to about $80,137 at level 8. Budgets are indexed each July and managed quarterly.

These are the standard rates for people who entered from 1 November 2025. People who had a Home Care Package before then may have different rates under the no worse off principle. A lifetime cap applies.
