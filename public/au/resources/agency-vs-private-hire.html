Agency or private hire: what is the difference?

A provider employs, screens, trains, insures and supervises its support workers and covers sick days. Hiring privately (directly or through a platform) can cost less per hour, but you take on more responsibility for checks, insurance, backup and, with Support at Home funding, rules about who can be paid.

Which to choose

If your mum has dementia, consistency, training and reliable backup usually matter more than the lowest hourly rate. If needs are light and you have time to manage, private hire can work well.

  • Ask who is liable if a support worker is injured in the home
  • Ask what happens on a sick day
  • Get everything in writing
