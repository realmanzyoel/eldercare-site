How to get a My Aged Care assessment

Call My Aged Care on 1800 200 422 or apply online. They ask questions by phone and, if eligible, arrange an assessor to visit at home. Have Medicare details, your doctor's contact and a family member present if possible.

The short answer

Call My Aged Care on 1800 200 422 or apply online. They ask questions by phone and, if eligible, arrange an assessor to visit at home. Have Medicare details, your doctor's contact and a family member present if possible.

Written by The Evenhome care teamClinical review pending: no article is published as medical guidance until a qualified reviewer signs it offLast updated 21 November 2026

Before the call

  • Medicare card
  • Doctor's details and medicine list
  • A list of what is hard day to day
  • Consent for a family member to speak for them

At the assessment

  • Be honest about bad days, not just good ones
  • Mention memory problems and falls
  • Mention the family carer's health
Older people talking around a table

After

You get a notice of decision. Keep it: it states the services and classification you are approved for.
