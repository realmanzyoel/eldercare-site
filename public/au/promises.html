The short answer

We make six promises we can measure. Each has a named owner, a monthly result and a stated remedy when we miss it. In Australia we are not providing care yet, so there are no results to show.

Promise 1

Care starts within 72 hours of signing

Owner
Operations lead
How we measure it
Hours from the signed care agreement to the GPS check-in of the first visit, for every new client.
Current result
Not yet measured: we are not providing care in Australia yet.
If we miss it
If we miss it, the first week's visits are credited at 10% off and the director calls you to explain why.

Promise 2

A replacement support worker within 48 hours, already introduced

Owner
Scheduling coordinator
How we measure it
Hours from a support worker becoming unavailable to a familiar backup checked in. We also count how often the replacement had met your family before.
Current result
Not yet measured: we are not providing care in Australia yet.

If we miss it, the hours of the missed shift are free and the director calls you.

Why publish this at all?

Families tell us the worst part of home care is not knowing. A promise without a number is a slogan. Publishing results, including bad months, is how we hold ourselves to it, and how you can check us.

  • Results come straight from our phone and scheduling systems
  • Bad months stay on the page
  • Each promise has one owner, not a committee
